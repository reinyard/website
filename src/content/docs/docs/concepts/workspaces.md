---
title: Workspaces and roles
description: How teams organize work and who can see what.
---

A **workspace** is where a team's agents, pipelines and runs live.

## Sign-in

Sign-in is **invite-only**. Nobody can create an account on their own.

## Roles

Members of a workspace have roles that determine what they can do in it.

## Administrators

Administrators can **see and manage every workspace**. Keep that in mind when deciding who should be an administrator. See the [Security model](/docs/security/).
